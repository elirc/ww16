# Trace lab

Trace a successful SUBMITTED to SALES_APPROVED request as rows: pre-read, policy, transaction begin, status update, approval insert, audit. Trace APPROVED separately and append job payload. Trace a missing deal and show that no transaction row exists. Trace update failure and audit failure with their last completed operation. Use the test's call records as the concrete trace. The pre-read is outside the transaction; inspect the resulting stale-read risk before proposing a change.

Build a worksheet with columns for operation, input, client identity, returned value and failure outcome. For sales approval, fill find, tx, update, approval and audit. For channel approval, append job and record its source id and quarter. The refusal matrix contains PARTNER_SELLER requesting sales approval (403), a direct SUBMITTED to APPROVED jump (409), and an absent visible deal (404). All three show find as the only recorded operation. In the update failure row, approval must be absent. In the audit failure row, job must be absent. Compare your worksheet to call arrays in the runtime lab, then mark each row as observed through an adapter or executed pure policy. This final annotation prevents a sequence diagram from becoming an unsupported claim about database durability.
