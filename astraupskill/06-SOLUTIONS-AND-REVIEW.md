# Solutions and review

Valid sales approval records update, approval, and audit with one transaction client. Channel approval adds the job only when next status is APPROVED. A wrong role or invalid edge stops before transaction. Missing deal produces not-found before policy. Update failure prevents approval and audit; audit failure rejects after earlier calls. Review should verify session.role, exact from/to history, tx reuse, and bounded claims about rollback and concurrency.

For Exercise A, history contains d1, u1, SUBMITTED, SALES_APPROVED and reviewed; no leaderboard job is created. Exercise B returns statusCode 403 before tx. Exercise C returns 409 for the invalid edge, also before tx. A missing visible deal returns 404. Exercise D stops at update for the first injected failure, and reaches update plus approval before failing at audit in the second; the APPROVED job is not reached. Exercise E must coordinate reads and writes with barriers, not sleeps, and verify persisted records from another connection. A version or expected-status predicate must be checked at mutation time to detect a stale observation. These are design exercises, not changes delivered by this course. Reviewers should distinguish an awaited transaction callback from an observed successful commit.
